It's graduation day at Riverview High, and red-headed Alex has a big decision to make: will he build his future with sweet girl-next-door Cathy, or with vivacious, wealthy Erica? It won't be easy, because Cathy and Erica have suitors of their own, each fighting to win the girl of his dreams.

Set in the mid-1990s, *Riverview High* follows a group of teenagers through the trials and tribulations of friendship, love and growing up. It's inspired by (and sometimes parodies) the teen sitcoms of the era, like *Saved by the Bell* and *California Dreams*.
